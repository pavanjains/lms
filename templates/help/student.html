Student manual

How to sign in, find your lessons and stay safe.

On this page
  • Signing in
  • Your workspace
  • Your assigned lessons
  • What the status means
  • Signing out and staying safe

Signing in

  1. Open the home page and choose Student sign in.
  2. Type the username and password your instructor gave you.
  3. Choose Sign in.

If the sign-in does not work, check your spelling and try again. If it still fails, or you have forgotten your password, ask your instructor for a new one. Your instructor cannot see your old password, but can give you a new one.

Your workspace

After you sign in, My workspace shows your name, your grade and the classes you are in. Use the Home and My workspace tabs at the top of any page to get back.

Your assigned lessons

Choose My assigned lessons on your workspace. The list shows each lesson's name, the class it belongs to, the robotics kit and your status.

  • Choose a lesson's name to read its instructions.
  • When a lesson has a link to LEGO building instructions, it opens in a new tab.
  • Lessons are for reading. There is nothing to submit and no button to mark a lesson finished.

If a lesson you expected is missing, ask your instructor.

What the status means

Your instructor keeps track of your progress and updates the status for you. Written feedback from instructors is for staff and is not shown on your pages.

Signing out and staying safe

  • Choose Sign out before the next student when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
  • For safety, you are signed out after a period without activity, so you may need to sign in again.
  • Never share your password or write it where others can see it.
  • If something looks wrong, tell your instructor.
